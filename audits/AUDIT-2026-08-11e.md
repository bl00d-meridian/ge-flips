# Audit — 2026-08-11e (decomposition primitive + three Trade sub-views)

Scope: the build shipping the shared decomposition primitive and the Paper Book,
Prospecting and Gate Health sub-views. Run before commit, per the standing discipline
(integration audit after any week containing a build session). Sections follow the ruled
order: workflow walks, orphan scan, redundancy scan, interrogability scan.

## 1 · Workflow walks

**Walk-up.** Untouched. The three surfaces render nothing on Home, present no rulings,
and add no checklist step. `walkupDecisionCount()` is asserted unchanged and still ≤ 7,
and the walk-up checklist is asserted to contain no `data-drill` face at all — the
expansions are a reading affordance and have no business inside an execution list.
Accrual is unchanged and still rides the poll: `[R34.1]` now runs its tab-independence
loop over the three new sub-views as well as the four top tabs.

**Weekly review.** Five surfaces changed shape. The paper-book, hours-ledger and
recipe-basis checklist steps and the audit's §4 gate health and §5 prospecting are now
one-line reads carrying their own `data-goto`. What feeds them: the same functions that
feed the sub-views (`shadowByGate`, `gateHealthAudit`, `hoursLedger`, `recipeBasis`,
`DB.strataStats`) — one computation, two readers, so a summary can never disagree with
the surface it summarises. What they feed: the decision to go look. When they earn their
render: every review, as the periodic status read.

**Sub-view entry.** Trade tab → sub-tab button, or any `data-goto="paper|prospect|gates#…"`
deep link (`setTab` accepts the three as top-level aliases exactly like the legacy
`watch`/`scan`/`log` names, so the deep links land on the sub-view and not merely on the
Trade tab). Each renders only in its own branch of `renderAll`, and `paintDrills()` runs
last so a 60s poll re-applies whatever the operator had open.

**Paper → probation funnel.** Unchanged and re-checked end to end: scanner cohort →
scout admission → watchlist → gates → probation → real size. Gate Health *reports* the
lane's standing; it deliberately carries no grant, revoke or re-affirm control, because
those rulings already have a home on the bench line, the row's expand view and the
rulings digest. Adding a fourth place to press them would have been a new decision surface
smuggled in as a report.

No missing answers. **No findings.**

## 2 · Orphan scan

Every symbol added by this build has a definition and at least one reader (checked
mechanically over the 35 new names). Two data fields were added and both are read:

- `p.exitPx` on a forced-exit paper record — written in `shadowTick`, read by
  `paperTripRows`. Before this, a forced exit rendered an outcome whose price did not
  exist anywhere; records closed earlier render "— not retained", never a guessed number.
- `gateHealthAudit().rows[].items`, `.dieOff.eps` and `.fundedItems` — written in the same
  single pass the aggregates come from, read by the Gate Health drill-downs.

`shadowAuditInline()` was **removed**, not left dangling: its three sections became
`paperRegimeSection` / `paperGateSection` / `paperCohortSection`, all rendered by the
sub-view, and the probe assertions that referenced it were re-pointed rather than dropped.

**No findings.**

## 3 · Redundancy scan

One overlap exists and is deliberate: per-gate paper outcomes render twice — as the
sortable ledger in Paper Book, and as the paper half of the two-stream tree in Gate
Health. This is user-directed (the two-stream pairing is the point of Gate Health), and
the divergence risk is structurally closed: both renderers read the same `shadowByGate()`
computation, so they cannot disagree. Recorded here so it is a known overlap rather than
a rediscovery next quarter.

**No findings; one deliberate overlap noted.**

## 4 · Interrogability scan

The scan the ruling exists for. Every aggregate on the three surfaces was checked for a
one-click path to its rows. Three numbers could not be opened; all three are fixed in
this commit:

| Finding | Surface | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| I1 | Prospecting — the per-stratum map's **Filled** column was a bare count | opens to the trips that would have filled, with the same durable-counter subset disclosure as its neighbours |
| I2 | Gate Health — **funded lines** count was a bare number with no rows anywhere | `gateHealthAudit` now returns `fundedItems` from its existing pass; the count opens to the passed items and what they traded afterwards |
| I3 | Gate Health — the paper stream's **"(N at 100%)"** reading was openable in the Paper Book table but not here | opens to the trips that clear without the participation haircut |

**Deliberate non-findings**, recorded with their reasoning so the next scan does not
re-raise them: a handful of *net* figures (cohort nets, per-stratum net where the closed
count sits beside it, hourly net beside the fill rate, gap-band net) render without their
own face. In each case the adjacent count opens the identical population, and every row in
that expansion carries its own net. The rule is that the rows must be one click away, not
that every glyph needs its own button; a second face onto the same rows would be noise,
not auditability. Where the population differs, the number has its own face.

**Honest-subset disclosure** was checked as its own property, because a truncated
expansion is the failure mode that looks like success. Two cases exist and both state
themselves: the per-stratum ledger's counters are durable by R29.4 and outlive the paper
book's 30-day pruning, so those expansions read "Showing 1 of 48 — … the paper book prunes
closed trips after 30 days"; and any expansion over 20 rows says "Showing the first 20 of
N" and offers the rest. A counter whose rows are *all* pruned renders words, not a blank
box. Both are probe-asserted (`[R35.2]`) and both were proven by seeding the defect.

## 5 · Verification integrity

Every new assertion was proven by seeding the defect it exists to catch and watching it
fail, per the standing practice. Seven defect runs covered: expansions present but
invisible (`display:none` on `.drillbox` — caught by the computed-visibility half, the
R22.2 shape); sub-view scoping broken two ways (`.tsub{display:block}`, and an ID rule
outranking `section.tab{display:none}` — the historical regression, caught by the Home and
Review absence assertions); `paintDrills()` dropped from `renderAll`; structural and
row-cap subset disclosure silenced; sort direction never flipping; the filter no longer
naming what it hides; the die-off ledger losing its rows; the exception lane losing its
void-basis label and its rows; two regime curves instead of three; the epoch banner
dropped; the cohort ledger dropping its empty populations; a sub-tab removed; a summary
line stopping linking through; the review re-rendering a table it should only link to; and
`drill()` rendering a bare number. Each failed with the intended assertion named, then
green was restored.

Two assertions were rewritten during that exercise because the seeding proved they
*could not* fail:

- **The hours-table assertion had an escape hatch.** It read "if the panel has hour data,
  check the two streams, else pass" — and the fixture had no hour data, so it passed
  vacuously. The fixture now seeds `roiHour` readings and the guard is gone.
- **The epoch-banner assertion was `!shadowEpochYoung() || …`** — vacuously true whenever
  the banner was not due. It now forces the young state, asserts, and restores.

Both are the R29.4 shape from the case law: a detector written for the right property with
a fixture that prevented the property from expressing itself.

**Open finding (verification, not product): the suite is intermittently clock-dependent.**
`[R18.1]` ("rescan while open opens nothing") and the three `[R20.5]` delta assertions
failed in 2 of ~14 runs with *identical* code, both times with extra discovery-slice
positions and extra clean-gate-passer delta lines. The discovery slice draws from one
stratum chosen by the price cycle, which is a function of `S.latestAt`, so which items the
slice sees — and therefore how many counterfactuals it opens — can vary between runs. Six
consecutive runs then passed, so it is rare. This is pre-existing and unrelated to this
build (reproduced with no edits between a failing and a passing run), but a suite whose
green sometimes depends on the clock is a green that claims more than it knows.

*Applied:* the `[R18.1]` failure extra now carries `S.latestAt`, the drawn stratum and the
slice-position count before and after the rescan, so the next occurrence reports its own
cause instead of reading like a dedup bug.

*Proposed, not applied* (fixture change touching the suite's global clock — the user's
ruling): pin `S.latestAt` in the fixture to a value whose price-cycle index is stable, so
the stratum rotation is deterministic across runs. Held back because pinning the clock
could shift staleness-sensitive assertions elsewhere in the suite, and that trade is worth
a look rather than a silent fix.

## 6 · Result

Four scans, **three interrogability findings, all fixed in this commit**; no orphan and no
workflow findings; one deliberate redundancy recorded; one open verification finding
instrumented and proposed. Suite green on both viewports (1200×900 and 390×844), 140
requirement ids asserted.
