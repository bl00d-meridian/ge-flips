## Item 1 — collapse first-tap bug: reproduced, actual cause stated

**Cause (neither suspect was quite it):** the control's *binding* was fine — all
collapse handlers are document-delegated — and its state initializes before first
render. The failure is between pointerdown and click: every render pass rebuilt the
checklist header via `innerHTML` even when the markup was byte-identical, and a
rebuild that lands inside the tap window (pointerdown on the old node, pointerup on
its replacement) produces **no click event at all**. Two rebuild sources made the
first interaction the likely victim: the boot data arrival re-renders Home about a
second after open — right when a walk-up's first tap lands — and the 60s poll
re-renders everything thereafter. "Works later or after reload" is exactly the
signature of a tap racing a rebuild.

**Fix:** `setHTML` — a content-diffed innerHTML write applied to the checklist
regions and Home blocks. Unchanged content keeps its DOM nodes, so taps land; the
race window now exists only for renders whose content genuinely changed. Probe
`[R22.1]` asserts node identity across a background re-render and a responding
first tap.

## Item 3 — ruling path, walked end-to-end

Walk: ⚖ badge (any tab) → Home → digest line → inline action → state change →
surface reflects it. Two composition regressions found and fixed:

**F16 — intel rulings from the Home digest landed invisibly.** The
ratify/edit-ratify/dismiss handlers re-rendered the Sleeve queue and (if visible)
the watchlist, but never the digest or the ⚖ badge — pre-reorg these handlers
served the Sleeve cards, and the reorg made the digest the primary ruling surface
without adding it to their render set. A ruling appeared to do nothing until the
next poll: "ratification actions not findable" was the ruled line still sitting
there after being ruled. *Fix:* all three handlers (and the dismiss undo) call
`rerenderCk()` + `updatePendBadge()`. Probe `[R22.3]`: the line leaves the digest
and the badge updates in the same interaction.

**F17 — audit deep-links landed unanchored.** "Rule it in the review's audit"
navigated to the Review tab top; the audit inline lives nine steps down. *Fix:*
links anchor to `review#ckPanel`. Every digest line's actions verified visible
inline (buttons, never hover-gated) — intel (3 actions), rung (sold…), coherence
(admit/dismiss), cluster (confirm/merge/dismiss), audit + funnel (anchored links),
all with snooze.

## Item 4 — auto-promote flow, walked end-to-end

**Finding: the recompute already had auto-promote semantics** — `buildPlan` refunds
from the ranked pass list up to slots/budget on every recompute, so a NEXT UP
gate-passer funds the moment capacity frees. What made it *feel* press-gated was
display, not mechanism: rows showed nothing about plan state, held items vanished
silently (skipped into no bucket), and the promote flow's "promoted and funded — no
demotion needed" success path read as if the press had been necessary when it had
only reordered budget allocation. The behavior is now **stated** (NEXT UP badges and
copy say "funds automatically at the next recompute; promote only jumps the ORDER")
and **probe-locked** (`[R22.5]`: capacity freed → the NEXT UP item is in `picks` at
the next `buildPlan` with `planPriority` untouched) so the semantics can't silently
regress. No allocator, cap, or gate changed.

**F18 — the silent unfunded states were the held and mm-owned items.** An item with
an open position passed gates and appeared in no bucket — no badge, no reason. The
status scheme names them ("live position", "mm cycle") along with every other state
(`[R22.4]`).

## Items 5–6 — beyond-net tags, four-slot rows

pending chart" states the honest limit (slice items don't fetch 7-day series — the

watched rows now show real plan status instead of the old one-gate "✗ gates"
mismatch badge, and their five caution badges collapsed into the chip.
