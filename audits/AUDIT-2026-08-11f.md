## 2 · Interrogability scan (re-run)

Three findings, all fixed in this commit — all of the same shape, and it is worth naming:
**the exclusion counts introduced by the observation floor were bare numbers.** A count
of what a verdict threw away is itself an aggregate, and "3 excluded" that cannot be
opened is exactly the number the operator cannot audit.

| Finding | Surface | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| I4 | Gate-health tree — the paper stream's excluded count | opens to the excluded trips |
| I5 | Cohort ledger — per-cohort excluded counts | opens to that cohort's excluded trips |
| I6 | Hours table — per-hour excluded counts | opens to that hour's excluded trips |

The fix was made in `thinNote()` itself rather than at the three call sites, so the next
aggregate that reports an exclusion inherits the drill-down instead of re-earning it —
the same reason the decomposition primitive exists.

New aggregates introduced this build and checked: the per-gate **Excluded** column, the
per-stratum **Excluded** column, the paper book's **Insufficient observation** line, and
the review's **Applied automatically** block — all open to their rows. The auto-applied
block additionally carries its undo path and the reason drops are excluded, so the block
answers "what happened and how do I reverse it" without leaving the page.

## 7 · The recipe-basis answer, recorded

Asked and answered before cutting. **What it measured:** the per-craft processing basis
on ten hand-picked recipes — output sold at its ask net of GE tax minus inputs bought at
their bids — with a daily ledger and a "basis break" at 2× a recipe's own median absolute
basis, attributing the widest-moving leg. **Why it was built:** a wide basis is
simultaneously a flip signal on the mispriced leg and a manipulation tell, since a pump
distorts one leg of a recipe before the other. **What decision it would change:** nothing,
as built. It sized nothing, capped nothing, benched nothing, and fed no gate, no scout
nomination and no paper trip — the break rendered a sentence, and acting on it meant
hand-carrying an item name to the watchlist. Its manipulation-tell half duplicated
`suspectedPump`, which is already wired to auto-arm restraint; ten hardcoded recipes is
also a fixed, tiny universe beside the stratified slice, which samples the whole item
space on a clock. Thin, so cut entirely rather than relocated. **If it returns it should
return wired** — a break nominating the mispriced leg into the scanner's paper cohort —
which is a new capability and needs its own price tag and ruling.
