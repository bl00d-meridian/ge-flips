## Provenance, stated before the table

The queue's "32 fill-tier CONFIRMED" roster is **not re-derivable**: the two-agent
verification syntheses died (M149), and the surviving census
(`CENSUS-2026-08-13-jobA-verification.md`) is the single-agent Job A record — its own
header says so. What survives is the census's **45 fill-tier findings, a superset that
contains every CONFIRMED member**, each with per-finding evidence and an unapplied fix.
This triage therefore covers the 45; the disposition of a finding depends on the code it
sits on, not on its confirmation status, so the superset is safe in the only direction
that matters — no CONFIRMED finding escapes a disposition. Where a fix is eventually
applied, the census's single-agent evidence is re-verified at fix time (the standing
seeding discipline does this anyway).

**Three dispositions, not two, deliberately.** Several findings assert a *property* the
constitution keeps on a *surface* the scorer supersedes. Forcing those into either bucket
would misfile them — the residue lesson: a real third outcome must not be crammed into a
two-way split. So: **TRANSFER** (the code ships into 1d's shared fill core; fix folded
into 1d), **EXPIRE-AT-RETIREMENT** (the code is ruled superseded; the finding dies with
its surface when the retirement ruling lands, and is deliberately not fixed in the
interim), **SPLIT** (the property re-lands in 1d/1e as a conformance obligation; the
specific assertion expires with its surface).
