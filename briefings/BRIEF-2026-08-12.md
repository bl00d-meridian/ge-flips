## ⚠ The finding that reframes the whole docket

**Reported GE volume stepped up ~5–7× across the entire market on 2026-07-24, and every
open anomaly flag's volume term is measuring that, not the market.**

Five control items, chosen to span unrelated market segments, each show a single-day,
sustained step in reported daily volume on 2026-07-24 with no matching price move:

| Control | Jul 1–23 avg/day | Jul 25–Aug 10 avg/day | Volume × | Price × |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nature rune | 3,581,016 | 26,354,072 | **7.4** | 1.17 |
| Shark | 611,393 | 3,456,011 | **5.7** | 1.05 |
| Ranarr weed | 134,606 | 815,449 | **6.1** | 1.02 |
| Dragon boots | 1,466 | 7,976 | **5.4** | 0.95 |
| Uncut sapphire | 235,751 | 1,318,659 | **5.6** | 0.94 |

The daily series make it a clean discontinuity, not a ramp — nature rune runs 0.64m–5.38m
every day through Jul 23, then 17.96m–33.82m every day from Jul 24 onward. Dragon boots:
260–2,139 through Jul 23, then 6,831–9,618. Prices do nothing.

A genuine 5–7× sustained increase in real trading across nature runes, sharks and dragon
boots simultaneously, with flat prices, is not a market event. The leading explanation is a
change in the coverage or aggregation of the OSRS Wiki real-time-prices volume feed on that
date. **No official source states this** — the measurement is confirmed and reproducible
from the cited endpoints; the cause is inferred and unsourced. I found nothing on the wiki's
Real-time Prices pages or in search announcing it.

**What it does to the flags.** The anomaly scan compares a recent window against a
2–3-weeks-ago baseline. Until roughly **2026-08-14** every such baseline straddles
2026-07-24, so every liquid item shows a several-hundred-percent volume gain by construction.
Normalised against the control band (×5.4–7.4), the flagged items are:

## Addendum — ruled the same day (2026-08-12, after this brief was written)

All four detector proposals were ruled and are now built. The brief above is left as
written; this records what changed.

1. **Normalisation shipped as the durable fix.** `VOL_INDEX_BASKET` — eight liquid,
   deliberately unrelated items — is recomputed at the start of every anomaly scan, and
   the scan's volume term is now item-volume-relative-to-index rather than raw. An item
   flags only when it beats the index by the same 25pp it previously had to beat its own
   past. The index renders on the scan panel and opens to its control rows.
2. **Cross-break suppression shipped as the interim**, and is date-driven: when an item's
   two windows straddle 2026-07-24 the volume term is suppressed and the item is NOT
   flagged, with the panel stating why. It expires on its own once baselines clear the
   break — no flag day to remember. **The scan still wants re-running after Aug 14**, on
   a fully post-break baseline, and what survives is the real docket.
3. **`measured` added to the confidence ladder.** Direct computation from the price API,
   reproducible from cited endpoints. It outranks `confirmed` for tape claims; `confirmed`
   stays top for announcements, enforced mechanically — a catalyst claiming `measured` is
   rejected at import rather than downgraded. The volume-break record is retagged
   `measured`.
4. **Three records dismissed**, carried in the file as `disposition` blocks with the
   artifact finding as the stated reason, decision-logged on import: **Bandos chestplate
   watch-note**, **Bandos tassets watch-note**, and the **Golem Crafting uncut gems
   deflation-flag**.

**A defect found while shipping the corrections, and fixed:** re-importing an
already-ratified record was silently absorbed, so the eleven corrected records this brief
